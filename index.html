Non Profit Organization

Our Mission

Youth Mock Trial ry is committed to advancing legal education for young people across Finland. Through structured programs, courtroom simulations, and national competitions, we provide students with the tools to understand justice, advocacy, and civic responsibility.

Programs & Initiatives

National Mock Trial Competition

Our flagship program brings together students from across the country to compete in realistic courtroom simulations.

Legal Education Workshops

We partner with legal professionals to deliver hands-on workshops in advocacy, evidence, and courtroom procedure.

School Partnerships

We collaborate with schools to integrate mock trial programs into their curriculum and extracurricular offerings.
