Youth Mock Trial ry

Empowering the next generation of legal professionals

Non Profit Organization

Our Mission

Youth Mock Trial ry is committed to advancing legal education for young people across Finland. Through structured programs, courtroom simulations, and national competitions, we provide students with the tools to understand justice, advocacy, and civic responsibility.

Who We Are

Youth Mock Trial ry is a national nonprofit dedicated to strengthening civic engagement and legal literacy among youth. We work with schools, legal professionals, and community partners to create accessible pathways into the world of law, advocacy, and justice.

What We Do

Our work centers on providing meaningful, hands‑on legal education experiences for young people across Finland. Through structured programming, professional mentorship, and national‑level competitions, we help students develop confidence, critical thinking, and advocacy skills.

  • Mock trial education and courtroom skills
  • Workshops led by legal professionals
  • National youth competitions
  • School partnerships and curriculum support

Helsinki Youth Mock Trial

Our flagship competition brings together motivated students from across Finland to engage in realistic courtroom simulations, develop advocacy skills, and experience the justice system firsthand.
