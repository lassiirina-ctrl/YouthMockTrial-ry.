What We Offer

Mock Trial Competitions

Structured courtroom simulations where students take on roles as lawyers, witnesses, and jurors.

Workshops & Training

Skill-building sessions in advocacy, public speaking, and legal reasoning for youth across Finland.

School Partnerships

Collaborations with schools to integrate mock trial and legal education into classroom activities.

Want to get involved in Youth Mock Trial ry?

Whether you are a student, teacher, legal professional, or community member, there are many ways to participate in Youth Mock Trial ry. Join us in expanding access to legal education and empowering the next generation of advocates.
