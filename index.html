Welcome

Youth Mock Trial ry is a national organization dedicated to providing young people with hands-on experience in law, advocacy, and courtroom procedure. Our mission is to make legal education accessible, engaging, and empowering. Join us as we shape the legal minds of tomorrow.

What We Offer

🏛️ National Competitions

Participate in our nationally-recognized mock trial competitions that challenge your legal knowledge, presentation skills, and courtroom expertise.

📚 Professional Training

Access comprehensive workshops and training programs led by experienced legal professionals and renowned educators in the field.

🤝 Strategic Partnerships

Connect with schools and youth organizations nationwide to expand your network and unlock new opportunities for growth.

⚖️ Real Courtroom Skills

Learn and practice authentic courtroom procedures, evidence examination, cross-examination, and legal argumentation techniques.
