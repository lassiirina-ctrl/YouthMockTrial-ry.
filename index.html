Welcome

Youth Mock Trial ry is a national organization dedicated to providing young people with hands-on experience in law, advocacy, and courtroom procedure. Our mission is to make legal education accessible, engaging, and empowering.

What We Offer

  • National-level mock trial competitions
  • Workshops and training for students
  • Partnerships with schools and youth organizations
  • Opportunities to learn real courtroom skills
