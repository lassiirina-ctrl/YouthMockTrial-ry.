Join Us

Get Involved

Youth Mock Trial ry welcomes students, volunteers, educators, and legal professionals who want to support youth legal education across Finland. Whether you want to participate, volunteer, or collaborate, there’s a place for you in our community.

Ways to Join

Student Participant

Join a team, compete in HYMT, and develop courtroom advocacy skills.

Volunteer Judge

Help evaluate student performances and support fair, educational competition.

School Partner

Bring mock trial programs to your school through workshops and competitions.

Community Volunteer

Assist with events, logistics, outreach, and youth engagement.

Membership

Becoming a member of Youth Mock Trial ry helps support our mission and ensures that our programs remain accessible to youth across Finland. Members receive updates, early registration access, and opportunities to participate in exclusive workshops.

Ready to Join?

Fill out our interest form to get started. We’ll contact you with next steps based on your role and availability.
