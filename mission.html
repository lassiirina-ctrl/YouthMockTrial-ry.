Our Mission

What Drives Us

Our mission is to empower young people with the knowledge, confidence, and skills needed to understand the justice system and participate meaningfully in civic life. We believe that legal literacy is a cornerstone of democracy — and that every young person deserves access to tools that help them think critically, advocate effectively, and engage responsibly.

Why Our Mission Matters

In a world shaped by laws, rights, and responsibilities, understanding how justice works is essential. Youth Mock Trial ry helps students explore real legal concepts through hands‑on learning, courtroom simulations, and mentorship from legal professionals. These experiences build confidence, leadership, and a deeper understanding of civic engagement.

Our Impact

Education

Hundreds of students each year gain access to legal learning opportunities.

Empowerment

Participants build advocacy skills, public speaking confidence, and critical thinking.

Community

We connect youth with lawyers, judges, and educators across Finland.
