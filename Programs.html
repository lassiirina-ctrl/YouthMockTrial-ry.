Programs & Initiatives

Explore the educational and competitive opportunities we offer to students across Finland.

⚖️ National Mock Trial Competition

Our flagship annual competition brings together teams from across Finland to participate in realistic courtroom simulations judged by legal professionals.

📚 Legal Education Workshops

Interactive workshops led by lawyers, judges, and law students covering advocacy, evidence, courtroom procedure, and legal reasoning.

🏫 School Partnerships

We collaborate with schools to integrate mock trial programs into their curriculum and extracurricular offerings.

🧑‍⚖️ Volunteer Judge Program

Legal professionals and law students can volunteer as judges, mentors, and evaluators for competitions and workshops.

🌍 Community Outreach

We work with youth organizations and community groups to expand access to legal education and civic engagement.
