Helsinki Youth Mock Trial

Overview

The Helsinki Youth Mock Trial (HYMT) is Finland’s premier youth mock trial competition, bringing together students from across the country to engage in courtroom advocacy, legal reasoning, and collaborative teamwork. HYMT provides a professional, structured environment where participants argue real cases, present evidence, and learn from legal experts.

Competition Structure

Preliminary Rounds

Teams compete in multiple rounds, presenting both prosecution and defense arguments.

Semi‑Finals

The top‑scoring teams advance to a knockout round judged by legal professionals.

Grand Final

The final two teams compete in a full courtroom simulation in Helsinki.

Professional Judges

Lawyers, judges, and law students evaluate performance, evidence use, and advocacy.

Eligibility

HYMT is open to:

• Secondary school students (ages 14–19)
• Independent teams or school‑based teams
• Teams of 6–12 participants
• No prior legal experience required
