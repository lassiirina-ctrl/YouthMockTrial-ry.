About Us

Who We Are

Youth Mock Trial ry is a national nonprofit dedicated to strengthening civic engagement and legal literacy among youth across Finland. We work with schools, legal professionals, and community partners to create accessible pathways into the world of law, advocacy, and justice.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower young people with the knowledge, confidence, and skills needed to understand the justice system and participate meaningfully in civic life. Through courtroom simulations, workshops, and national competitions, we help students develop critical thinking, advocacy, and leadership abilities.

Our Values

Integrity

We promote fairness, honesty, and respect in all aspects of our work.

Education

We believe legal literacy is essential for empowered citizenship.

Community

We collaborate with schools, families, and legal professionals to support youth.
